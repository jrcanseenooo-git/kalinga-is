No signal - keep working in Field mode

This page needs internet, but Kalinga's Field mode works without signal on this device.

  • Do a new case intake
  • Continue intakes you saved earlier
  • Add progress notes to cases you downloaded

Open Field mode

Field mode opens with your field passphrase, so you do not need to sign in. Everything you save is locked on this device and sent to Kalinga automatically when signal returns.

No signal right now

Field mode is not set up on this device yet, so Kalinga can't open without internet here.

Next time you have signal, set it up once:

  1. Sign in to Kalinga.
  2. Open Field mode in the menu.
  3. Create a field passphrase and download your cases.

After that, intakes and case notes work even in areas with no signal.
