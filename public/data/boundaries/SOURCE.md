# Administrative boundaries

Low-resolution TopoJSON (0.1% of vertices), PSGC 3Q 2026, from
https://github.com/faeldon/philippines-json-maps (release `topojson-lowres.zip`,
SHA-256 68321d0513148dca7fda4610d84d5b351bbd005dc293bb73175811befed355af).
MIT License, Copyright (c) James Faeldon. Boundaries derive from PSA / NAMRIA
public data.

Hosted by Kalinga so that no outside map service sees which areas users view.
Files are loaded one area at a time by the analytics map.

- `country/` regions of the Philippines
- `regions/` provinces (and NCR districts) of one region
- `provdists/` municipalities and cities of one province or district
- `municities/` barangays of one municipality or city
